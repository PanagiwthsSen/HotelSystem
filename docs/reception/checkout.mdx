# Check-Out

The **Check-out** page lists all guests departing today. It includes room charges and any mini-bar consumption, and generates a PDF receipt on completion.

## How to Check Out a Guest

1. From the sidebar, click **Αναχωρήσεις (Check-out)**. A table shows all reservations with **Check-out date** equal to today.

2. Review the guest's details:
   - **Customer name**
   - **Room number**
   - **Mini-bar** — a pill badge shows `OK` (no charges) or the amount and item details.
   - **Υπόλοιπο** — the total balance: room cost plus mini-bar charges.

3. Click the **Check-out** button. A receipt modal titled **Απόδειξη Εξόδου** appears.

4. Review the receipt. It contains:
   - Guest name, room number, check-in / check-out dates, and number of nights.
   - Payment method (cash, card, or bank transfer).
   - A breakdown of charges:
     - **Διαμονή** — the room cost.
     - **Mini-bar** — itemised list with quantities and amounts (if any).
   - The **Σύνολο** total.

5. Click **Επιβεβαίωση & PDF** to finalise. The system:
   - Marks the reservation as **CheckedOut**.
   - Marks the room as **dirty** (ready for housekeeping).
   - Downloads a **PDF receipt** automatically.

6. The badge changes to **Ολοκλ.** and the button becomes **C/O OK** (disabled).

<Note>
  If the PDF library fails, a plain-text (`.txt`) receipt is downloaded as a fallback. You can also manually export the receipt by clicking **Απόδειξη (PDF)** in the page header.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Check-out is **final**. Once confirmed, the reservation cannot be re-opened. The room is immediately queued for cleaning and will appear as **dirty** on the room map.
</Warning>

## Mini-Bar Charges

- Any mini-bar consumption recorded for the reservation is automatically included in the total.
- Each item is listed by name with its quantity and charge.
- The receipt shows a subtotal for mini-bar charges and a combined total with the room cost.
- Mini-bar charges are refreshed automatically every 20 seconds, so recent additions appear without manual reloading.

## What Happens in the Background

- The `RESERVATION` record is updated to status `CheckedOut`.
- The `ROOM` record is updated to status `dirty` (needs cleaning).
- A receipt PDF (or `.txt` fallback) is generated and downloaded.
- The dashboard counters (occupancy, departures, free rooms) update immediately.
